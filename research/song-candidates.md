# Swedish public-domain Christmas songs: candidates

Candidates for the default `songs.json`, all in Swedish, with lyrics believed to be in the public domain.

**Rule used:** everyone who wrote the Swedish lyrics, or translated them, died in 1955 or earlier
(Sweden: 70 years after death), and the text was first published in 1930 or earlier (US, where
GitHub Pages is hosted). Traditional songs with no known author qualify. The music doesn't matter
here, since the game only shows words.

**Sources:** every text and first line comes from [sv.wikisource.org](https://sv.wikisource.org/wiki/Kategori:Julsånger),
which only hosts out-of-copyright texts. Death years come from Wikisource author pages and
Swedish Wikipedia. "Known" is my own rough guess at how many Swedes would recognise the song.
The five words avoid giving away the title where possible. Change them freely.

**Try them:** in the game, open **Redigera låtar → Ladda upp fil (lägg till)** and pick
[`song-candidates.json`](song-candidates.json). It contains the 43 songs in the first table. Your list then lives
in your browser; **Återställ standardlistan** undoes it.
